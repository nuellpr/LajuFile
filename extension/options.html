LajuFile Capture

Send downloads from Chrome, Brave, Edge, or any Chromium browser to the LajuFile desktop app.

Pairing

In LajuFile, open Settings → Browser capture and copy the port and token. Port changes apply after restarting LajuFile.

How capture works

LajuFile captures normal HTTP/HTTPS downloads and link downloads. Forms, browser-protected streams, and downloads started before LajuFile is running stay in the browser. Cookies are sent only for the download URL.
